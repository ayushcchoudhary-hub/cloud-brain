# Shadow Fleet Size Estimates: How Many Tankers, How Much Oil

> How big the shadow tanker fleet is according to S&P Global, Lloyd's List Intelligence, KSE Institute and CREA, why their numbers differ so much, and what share of Russian seaborne crude it carries.

Research notes from public sources, as of November 2026.

## Why the numbers disagree

There is no official count of the shadow fleet. Each analyst uses its own definition, and the counts swing by hundreds of ships depending on three choices:

- **Which countries.** Some counts cover only Russian trade. Others add tankers that serve Iran and Venezuela.
- **Fleet vs active.** A count of every tanker linked to sanctioned trade is much larger than a count of tankers that loaded Russian oil in a given month.
- **What makes a tanker "shadow".** Most definitions rest on ownership, management, flag and insurance outside the G7+. Some also count any tanker that is sanctioned on the loading date.

So a figure of 176 and a figure of 1,423 can both be correct. They answer different questions.

## The main estimates

**S&P Global (3 September 2025).** 978 tankers of about 127 million dwt, or roughly 18.5% of the global oil tanker fleet. That was up from 889 tankers in November 2024. S&P split the total by trade: 561 tankers in Russian trade, 170 Iranian, 54 Venezuelan, and 193 "flexible" ships that switch between sanctioned trades.

**Lloyd's List Intelligence (reported by Reuters, 15 December 2025).** 1,423 tankers carrying sanctioned oil from Russia, Iran and Venezuela. Of those, 921 were under US, UK or EU sanctions. 702 were crude tankers, and 148 of those were not sanctioned. For scale, market estimates put the non-sanctioned global crude and fuel tanker fleet at about 9,000 vessels.

**KSE Institute (Russian Shadow Fleet Tracker, June 2026).** A narrower, Russia-only, monthly count. 176 shadow tankers were active in June 2026, or 54% of the 325 unique tankers used for Russian oil exports that month. 129 of the 176 (73%) made voyages while under EU sanctions. KSE defines the shadow fleet as tankers with no G7+ links for ownership, management or flag, and with no cover from the International Group of P&I Clubs.

**CREA (monthly analysis, September 2026).** CREA counts a tanker as "shadow" if it was sanctioned on the loading date, or was not owned or insured in G7+ countries.

## Share of Russian seaborne crude

The estimates of share agree more closely than the vessel counts do:

| Source | Period | Share of Russian seaborne crude on shadow tankers |
|---|---|---|
| S&P Global | "recent quarters" to Sep 2025 | 60% to 80% |
| UK government | March 2026 | around 75% |
| KSE Institute | July 2026 | about 68% by inference (IG P&I-insured tankers carried 32%) |
| CREA | September 2026 | 70% (67% sanctioned shadow, 3% non-sanctioned shadow) |

Oil products are different. In July 2026, KSE found IG P&I-insured tankers still carried 77% of Russian product exports. In September 2026, CREA found G7+ tankers carried 74% of products.

In absolute terms, S&P said the Russia-linked sanctioned fleet carried 3.3 million b/d of oil in the first half of 2025, of which 2.5 million b/d was crude. KSE counted 2,770 kb/d of crude on shadow tankers in July 2026, down from 2,870 kb/d in June.

## Iran and Venezuela

S&P, citing CAS data, estimated Iran's seaborne crude and condensate exports at about 1.6 million b/d in 2024 and 2025, up from 434,000 b/d in 2020. Venezuela's crude exports averaged 711,000 b/d in 2025 up to September. In early 2026, Kpler described Venezuela's "effective removal from sanctioned trade networks" and a collapse in shadow VLCC employment in January 2026 (see [[market-effects-and-buyers]]).

## How to use these numbers

- Always quote the source, the date and the definition together.
- Use fleet counts (S&P, Lloyd's List) for the scale of the whole parallel fleet. Use monthly trackers (KSE, CREA) for trends in Russian flows.
- Sanctioned share keeps rising as more ships are listed (see [[sanctions-enforcement]]). Operating patterns are in [[how-the-shadow-fleet-operates]].
